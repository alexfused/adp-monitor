Privacy policy

ADP Monitor for iPhone and iPad, by Alex Parkin (Parkin Systems). Last updated 19 October 2026.

The short version

ADP Monitor does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We have no server that your data passes through.

What the app handles, and where it stays

  • Your Macs' readings and screens. The app connects directly to your own Macs and shows their status and, if you choose, their screen. This travels only between your device and your Macs, encrypted with Apple's built-in encryption. It is not sent to us or anyone else and is not stored after you close the app.
  • Fleet key. The pairing key that lets your device talk to your Macs is stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only.
  • Camera. Used only to scan the pairing code your Mac shows. No images are saved or sent.
  • Local network. Used only to reach your own Macs.
  • Files and clipboard. Files you choose to send, and clipboard text if you turn clipboard sharing on, go only between your device and your Mac.

Children

The app is a technical tool and is not directed at children.
